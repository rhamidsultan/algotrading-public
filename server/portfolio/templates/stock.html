Corporate actions

    Entered by hand from company announcements and applied on the date shown. Later ones are not included until they are added.

    About these figures
    • For a stock you hold, the figures at the top are Zerodha's own. The graphs are rebuilt from your tradebook and the corporate actions.
    • Money invested is the cost of the units still held, oldest units sold first. A buy and a sell of the same stock on the same day cancel each other first, as Zerodha treats them. Brokerage, taxes and other charges are not included.
    • Dividends and cash paid for fractional shares are not counted in profit or loss.
    • After a merger or demerger, cost moves between stocks without new money, so the invested line can jump with no bar in "Added".
    • Values before a split or bonus are restated to the share count of the time. Around a demerger, past prices may be adjusted by the data provider, so values before that date are approximate.
    • This is for tracking only and is not tax advice. For tax filing, use Zerodha Console's tax P&L report.

    Import tradebook
    Equity tradebook files from Zerodha Console (Reports → Tradebook), Excel or CSV. Trades already stored are skipped; new trades are also recorded automatically each trading day.
